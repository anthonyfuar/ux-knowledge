# Business cost of deceptive design

## Use when
- Deceptive pattern proposed / found in review → need business case against it.
- Honest-design decision needs evidence.
- Covers settlements, penalties, refund + subscription effects, boycotts, market reactions, regulation, professional norms; each row → design consequence.
- Backs principles: Transparent, Respectful, Intentional.
- Before quoting any figure → check traps + limits in citation-caveats.md.

## The pattern behind every case
Through-line across all settlement cases below: short-term gain → reversal.

|Finding|Study (year, kind, sample)|Strength + caveat|Design consequence|
|---|---|---|---|
|Deceptive growth = spectacular short term → reverses via lawsuits, fines, refund spikes + reputational collapse|Ethical-design literature|Expert synthesis|Treat trust as business asset|
|Honest design → competitive edge as ad-blocking, tracking protection + privacy law spread|↑|↑|Define success so deception-driven lift = failure|
