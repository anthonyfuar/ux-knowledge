## Breaches, repurposing and shifting contexts

## Surveillance and power

|Finding|Study (year, kind, sample)|Strength + caveat|Design consequence|
|---|---|---|---|
|Luxury surveillance = consumer tracking devices people pay for + feel as benefit, yet functionally similar to ankle monitors|2022 essay by surveillance scholar|Term|Ask who bears cost once tracking becomes normal|
|1970s Detroit surveillance unit killed 22 people in 2.5 years, 21 of them Black|Historical case|Documented case|↑|
|Scant evidence city cameras, facial recognition / gunshot detection cut crime|—|Scant evidence|Refuse always-on background data collection|

Surveillance capitalism (as defined in 2019 book) = claiming human experience as raw material for predicting + modifying behavior → weakens independent thought. Argument in 4 steps:
1. Self-government impossible w/o both moral judgement + critical thinking.
2. Moral judgement + critical thinking depend on independence of thought + action.
3. This independence stripped once conduct tuned at scale by small, unnoticed penalties + rewards.
4. Information + influence bought this way concentrate to unmatched degree → what remains of self-government erodes.

Endpoint as argument pictures it: people merged into one collective apparatus whose workings enrich someone else.
- Protect people's capacity to reason + judge.
- Suggest; do not decide for person.
- Treat concentrated behavioral data as political risk, not only privacy issue.
