# Glossary A-C

Usability + humane-design terms, A to C. Open when term needs exact meaning or finding/rationale needs exact wording.

## Terms
|Term|Meaning|See|
|---|---|---|
|Three-gate check|Decision's 3 gates: technically possible? legally + strategically permitted? ethically right? Only 3rd (should) = actual ethical dilemma|../methods/three-gate-check.md (how to run gates)|
|Abusability|How easily one person can use product to harm another; presented as usability's successor as design concern|[Principles index](../principles/index.md)|
|Additive bias|People overlook subtractive changes + solve problems by adding instead; documented 2021|—|
|Aesthetic-Usability Effect|Attractive design judged easier to use → problems can go unnoticed in testing + small flaws treated more leniently|../laws/aesthetic-usability-effect.md|
|Alert fatigue|Once false positives common, people dismiss warnings → safety alerts must separate something moving w/ you from something merely near you|—|
|All caught up indicator|Visible signal: no more new content, fine to stop|[Principles index](../principles/index.md)|
|Anchoring|Too much weight on first number/fact seen + too little adjustment from it, even when irrelevant|../laws/cognitive-bias.md|
|Anticipatory design|Products predict what users want + decide for them → fewer choices; goes further than defaults|—|
|Apparent vs inherent usability|Perceived vs measured ease of design|—|
|Associative-deficit hypothesis|Aging weakens item-context binding → shrinks memory benefit of isolation (Von Restorff Effect); dates to 2000|../laws/von-restorff-effect.md|
|Attention economy|Market: products compete for scarce human attention + monetize people's time + data|—|
|Attenuation (attention theory)|Unattended input weakened, not blocked|../laws/selective-attention.md|
|Augmentation, intelligence (intelligence augmentation)|Standard: technology should raise people's capacity to understand + solve problems, not replace it|humane-design-ideas.md|
|Automation bias|Over-trusting automated output even when wrong, to point of overriding correct human judgment; vigilance drops after run of correct automated results|—|
|Automation complacency (out-of-the-loop, handover problem)|More reliable automation → people less ready to take over when it fails → design takeover moment + keep people's skills practised|—|
|Autoplay|Next item starts w/o deliberate choice; humane replacement = up-next prompt|[Principles index](../principles/index.md)|
|Banner blindness|Content unread when in ad position, beside ad or ad-like. Faux ad = legitimate content contrasting w/ context (often on mobile) + skipped as if ad|../laws/selective-attention.md|
|Bias blind spot|Seeing bias in others more readily than in oneself|../laws/cognitive-bias.md|
|Bit|Amount of information to pick one of two equally likely options; choosing among 16 equally likely alternatives takes 4 bits|../laws/hicks-law.md|
|Bottomless bowl|Bounded experience made endless → consumption continues past satiety; model for infinite feeds|../ethics/attention-hijacks.md|
|Calm technology|Technology in attention's periphery; informs w/o overburdening + recedes when not needed|[Calm technology patterns](../patterns/calm-technology-and-device-etiquette.md)|
|Captology|Study of computers as persuasive technologies|—|
|Change aversion|Short-term satisfaction drop after familiar interface redesigned; partly from status quo bias + mere-exposure effect|—|
|Change blindness|When cue faint or other changes compete, even major interface change goes unnoticed|—|
|Channel capacity|Bits channel can carry reliably per unit time; beyond it, judgments begin to fail|—|
|Choice Overload (overchoice)|Too many options overwhelm people, sour whole experience + slow decisions; satisfaction = inverted U as option count grows|../laws/choice-overload.md|
|Choice tax / decision fatigue|Each added option costs attention; piled-up decisions degrade judgment + push people toward defaults. Full entry: Decision fatigue in D-F|glossary-d-f.md|
|Chunking|Binding loose information fragments into units, each meaningful on its own|../laws/chunking.md|
|Closure|One of five Gestalt grouping families|../laws/gestalt-principles-overview.md|
|Cocktail party phenomenon|Following one conversation among many; studied w/ dichotic listening|../laws/selective-attention.md|
|Cognitive bias|Systematic, often unconscious judgment error from mental shortcuts|../laws/cognitive-bias.md|
|Cognitive load|Thinking capacity consumed by interface: figuring it out + working it|../laws/cognitive-load.md|
|Cognitive load effects|Split-attention effect = extra load from integrating information from separate sources. Worked-example effect = for novices, studying solved examples beats solving problems unaided. Expertise reversal effect = support helping novices hinders experts. Modality effect = spreading information across sight + hearing can reduce load|../laws/cognitive-load.md|
|Command-based interaction|GUI paradigm: system gives feedback in response to user's actions|—|
|Common fate|Elements moving together (same direction + speed) seen as one group even when far apart|../laws/gestalt-principles-overview.md|
|Common region|One sharply drawn enclosure (border/background) makes everything inside look grouped|../laws/common-region.md|
|Complexity bias|Complicated-looking answers seem expert → people favor them over plain ones. Diagnostic: pull toward more intricate option → problem itself not grasped yet|../laws/occams-razor.md|
|Confirmation bias|Existing belief steers what information gets sought, interpreted + remembered, toward confirming it|../laws/cognitive-bias.md|
|Consequence scanning|Recurring team ritual listing product's intended + unintended effects against team's stated values|organizations-and-toolkits.md|
|Contact patch / centroid|Finger's area on touch screen; sensor reduces it to single point|../laws/fittss-law.md|
|Context collapse|Separate social contexts flattened into one feed → content arrives w/o cues needed to interpret it|../evidence/filter-bubbles-and-polarization.md|
|Contextual inquiry|Observing + interviewing people while they do real tasks in own setting; catches work-arounds + context lab studies miss|—|
|Continuity (good continuation)|Elements along line/smooth curve grouped together; where lines cross, eye follows smoothest path|../laws/gestalt-principles-overview.md|
|Control continuum|Spectrum: full automation to full manual control. Product's place depends on device, task frequency + complexity, user expertise, product positioning + tone|—|
|Counter-metric|Measure beside growth metric, tracking harm that metric could cause|../methods/counter-metrics.md (how to pair them)|
|Cradling|Phone held in one hand while other steadies it or reaches across it|../patterns/calls-to-action-and-touch-targets.md|
|Cumulative layout shift|Web metric summing unexpected page-element movement over page's life|—|
