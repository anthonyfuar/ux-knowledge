# Misquoted claims

What each UX law/finding often gets misquoted, misdated or misread as vs what research showed + when. Cite by year, work type + finding.

## Use when
- Before naming year/term in review, design rationale or stakeholder argument.
- To tie law to right origin, date it + pick precise term.
- Why bother: citing law's origin year to stakeholders turns design preference into defensible argument.

## Before citing
1. Find claim in tables below.
2. Match year to specific claim, never to body of work it gets loosely credited to.
3. If source = essay, single field study or mostly animal work → say so next to claim.
4. If wording unverified → paraphrase constraint; never quote as verbatim saying.
5. Check year against timeline.md; pick exact term in glossary.md.

## Simplicity + reasoning
|Claim|Often misquoted as|Actually dated/shown|
|---|---|---|

## Check questions
- Each year matched to specific claim, not to whole body of work it gets loosely credited to?
- Source type stated where it limits claim (humorous essay, single field study, mostly animal studies, TV dramatization)?
- Common Region + Uniform Connectedness kept apart; Closure not credited to 1994 paper?
- Unverified maxim paraphrased, not quoted?
- 1989 reasoning study cited for task-sensitive reasoning, not as proof of complexity bias?
- Paper behind Miller's Law cited as 1956; its seven not used as cap?
