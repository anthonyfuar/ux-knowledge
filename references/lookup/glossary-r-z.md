# Glossary R-Z

Usability + humane-design terms, Q to Z (no term begins w/ Q). Open when term needs exact meaning or finding/rationale needs exact wording.

## Terms
|Term|Meaning|See|
|---|---|---|
|Rage click|Repeated rapid clicks on one element; in analytics, signals frustration/broken control|—|
|Read-tap asymmetry|Text readable on touch screen may prove too small to tap accurately|../patterns/calls-to-action-and-touch-targets.md|
|Recognition over recall|Recognizing seen items costs less effort than recalling them → show visited state + breadcrumbs|../patterns/navigation-and-menus.md|
|Reference class forecasting (outside view)|Estimating outcome from outcomes of similar past cases|—|
|Response-time terms|Think time = interval from receiving system's full reply to issuing next command; grows w/ system delay. Psychological closure = felt sub-goal completion; after it, delay tolerable. Step-down discontinuity = abrupt, non-linear mental-efficiency drop when delay crosses threshold. Psychological present = window of roughly 2.3–3.5 s felt as now|../laws/doherty-threshold.md|
|Roach motel|Easy to get into, hard to get out of; archetypal cancellation trap|../ethics/deceptive-patterns.md|
|Saccadic masking|Little taken in during rapid eye movement → hides changes made during it|—|
|Schema|Organized knowledge structure letting many elements act as one|../laws/cognitive-load.md|
|Search template|Mental image of target held during search; matching items capture attention|../laws/selective-attention.md|
|Second-order thinking|Asking "and then what?" repeatedly to trace consequences for indirect + direct users|../methods/second-order-thinking.md (how to run it)|
|Selective attention|Limited attention goes to stimuli serving current goal|../laws/selective-attention.md|
|Serial Position Effect|First + last items of sequence remembered best: early items well (primacy), final items most easily (recency)|../laws/serial-position-effect.md|
|Similarity|Elements alike in movement, orientation, size, shape or color grouped together, even when apart|../laws/similarity.md|
|Similarity-based interference|Holding + processing same kind of material at once conflicts most|../laws/working-memory.md|
|Skeuomorphism|Real-world visual metaphors bootstrap mental models for new interactions|../laws/mental-model.md|
|Smart defaults|Presets absorbing complexity on user's behalf; automation depends on them|../laws/teslers-law.md|
|Snapshot model|In hindsight, people do not weigh episode's whole course; a few typical, high-intensity moments decide verdict|../laws/peak-end-rule.md|
|Sneaking / preselection|Deceptive pattern adding or pre-checking paid items user did not choose|../ethics/deceptive-patterns.md|
|Speed bump (manual or algorithmic)|Visible confirmation or system-level slowdown limiting errors + abuse|../patterns/friction-undo-and-confirmation.md|
|Speed-accuracy trade-off|Error rate rises as movements get faster + targets smaller|../laws/fittss-law.md|
|Status quo bias|Outsized preference for current state or preset option|../laws/cognitive-bias.md|
|Status signals|Graded family of calm communication: status indicator = light, shape or position showing state; status tone = brief sound marking change; haptic alert = private nudge nobody else notices; status shout = deliberately unmissable alarm, reserved for danger. Rule: choose quietest status signal still carrying meaning|[Calm technology patterns](../patterns/calm-technology-and-device-etiquette.md)|
|Steering law|Time to move along constrained path grows w/ path length divided by width|../laws/fittss-law.md|
|Stimulus-response compatibility|Responses mapping naturally onto signal = faster|—|
|Surrogation|Treating metric as if identical to goal it stands for|../methods/counter-metrics.md|
|Surveillance capitalism|Human experience claimed as raw material for prediction + behavior modification. Shaping behavior at scale removes independence moral judgement + critical thought rest on; information + influence collect in very few hands → names political as much as economic condition: public not able to judge for itself cannot govern itself|humane-design-ideas.md|
|Temporal monotonicity|Assumption: adding discomfort always makes experience worse; peak-end studies contradict it|../laws/peak-end-rule.md|
|Temporal pointing|Hitting target available only during time window|—|
|Tesler's Law (waterbed theory)|Every system holds irreducible complexity; can only move between system + user; pressed down in one place, it rises in another|../laws/teslers-law.md|
|Time well spent|Design standard asking whether technology serves people's own values, not engagement|[Principles index](../principles/index.md)|
|Top-task analysis (long neck)|Users rank top tasks; a few tasks take most votes|../methods/top-tasks-and-user-problem-matrix.md|
|Transactive memory / digital amnesia|Remembering where information gets kept, not information itself|—|
|Transfer of learning; external consistency|Skills carry over from other products when features behave identically → external consistency: match conventions across products, not only within your own. Memory activation race = competing remembered solutions race each other + most practiced one tried first|../laws/jakobs-law.md|
|Transposition error|Two neighbouring items swap places in recalled sequence|../laws/working-memory.md|
|Tunnel vision|Task-focused gaze stays on region judged relevant → even large elements right outside it go unseen|../laws/selective-attention.md|
|UI accelerator|Shortcut for experts; novices do not see it|—|
|Uniform connectedness|Visible link between elements (arrows, frames, color, lines) makes them seem more strongly related|../laws/uniform-connectedness.md|
|Usage awareness|Showing people their own time + frequency of use, aiming at healthier habits|[Principles index](../principles/index.md)|
|User interview|One-to-one conversation on goals, habits + pain points → ask neutral, open questions + weigh answers against behavioral data|—|
|User-by-problem matrix|Grid: participants vs usability problems, to rank problems by how often they occur|../methods/top-tasks-and-user-problem-matrix.md|
|Value tension|Conflict between legitimate values; decision must resolve it explicitly. Hypothetical: school learning app deciding whether to show pupils class ranking (motivation vs pupils' self-esteem)|[Principles index](../principles/index.md)|
|Weapon focus|Vivid element captures memory at expense of ordinary details beside it|—|
|Working memory|Small, fast-decaying workspace holding 4–7 chunks, each lasting about 20–30 seconds|../laws/working-memory.md|
|Working-memory components|Central executive = steers attention, suppresses distraction + coordinates two tasks. Phonological loop = rehearses speech-like material. Visuospatial sketchpad = holds images, shapes + locations. Episodic buffer = binds sight, sound, space + meaning into one episode; from 2000. Focus of attention = single item currently selected for processing|../laws/working-memory.md|
|Zeigarnik Effect|Tasks broken off or left open remembered better than completed ones. Ovsiankina effect = urge to resume interrupted/unfinished task|../laws/zeigarnik-effect.md|
|Zombie scrolling|Trance-like scrolling w/ no destination, past point of value, often late at night|../patterns/feeds-scrolling-and-pacing.md|
