# Glossary G-M

Usability + humane-design terms, G to M (no term begins w/ K). Open when term needs exact meaning or finding/rationale needs exact wording.

## Terms
|Term|Meaning|See|
|---|---|---|
|Gestalt principles|Rules perception uses to organize stimuli into patterns + wholes|../laws/gestalt-principles-overview.md|
|Goal-distance model|Effort depends on share of original distance still left: smaller share left → more effort. Illusionary goal progress = head start shrinks share left while real work stays same → effort rises. Postreward resetting = effort drops after reward earned, then re-accelerates toward next goal|../laws/goal-gradient-effect.md|
|Goal-Gradient Effect|Closer goal → stronger drive to reach it|../laws/goal-gradient-effect.md|
|Gulf of evaluation|Gap between system state + person's ability to judge whether goal got met|—|
|Gulf of execution|Gap between what person intends + actions system makes available|—|
|Halo effect|One salient trait colors judgments of unrelated traits; explained as drive toward one coherent impression. Hypothetical: bank branch w/ polished lobby assumed to give more reliable loan advice|../laws/aesthetic-usability-effect.md|
|Happy path|Minimum-viable-product culture's default target: flow's run where nothing goes wrong, travelled by least vulnerable users|—|
|Hero control|Most used, most important or most drastic control; deserves prominence|../patterns/calls-to-action-and-touch-targets.md|
|Heuristic|Rule of thumb; speeds judgment but can add systematic error. UX laws themselves = design heuristics, not mandates|../laws/index.md|
|Heuristic evaluation|Usability inspection method|../checklists/usability-heuristics-map.md|
|Hick's Law (Hick-Hyman Law)|More choices + more complex choices → longer decision; formula RT = a + b log2(n)|../laws/hicks-law.md|
|Hook model|Habit-forming loop: trigger → action → variable reward → investment|../patterns/habits-streaks-and-gamification.md|
|Human face / machine face|Application's forgiving user-facing layer vs its strict, validating system-facing layer|../laws/postels-law.md|
|Human in the loop|People can see, correct + override automated decisions affecting them|[Principles index](../principles/index.md)|
|Idleness aversion|People dread idleness + want to stay busy → fill unavoidable waits w/ information/light entertainment; documented 2010|../patterns/feedback-loading-and-performance.md|
|Inclusive design|Way of working; scope = every kind of difference among people; accessibility = result of inclusive design, not substitute for it|[Principles index](../principles/index.md)|
|Index of difficulty (ID)|Fitts's Law bits measure of aimed-movement difficulty; depends only on distance-to-width ratio. Throughput = pointing performance in bits per second, adjusted for accuracy; standard metric for comparing input devices. Effective width = target width computed from where hits land, replacing nominal width|../laws/fittss-law.md|
|Indirect users|People affected by product they never use. Hypothetical: neighbours + delivery couriers recorded by household's video doorbell|../methods/second-order-thinking.md|
|Information scent|Cues signaling whether region likely serves user's goal|../patterns/navigation-and-menus.md|
|Intent-based interaction|System handles procedure; person states desired result in natural language|../patterns/ai-and-automated-features.md|
|Intentionality|Foresight about long-term + indirect consequences, as good-design criterion|[Principles index](../principles/index.md)|
|Interaction cost|Total physical + mental effort to reach goal|—|
|Intermittent variable reward|Unpredictable payoff after action; slot-machine core of compulsive checking|../evidence/addiction-and-variable-rewards.md|
|Invisible until needed|Staying unobtrusive until summoned, while still extending what person can do|[Principles index](../principles/index.md)|
|Isolation effect|Other name for Von Restorff Effect|../laws/von-restorff-effect.md|
|Jakob's Law|People expect your product to work like products they use most|../laws/jakobs-law.md|
|Late selection|Attentional selection happens after full meaning processing|../laws/selective-attention.md|
|Law of triviality (bike-shedding)|Time spent on agenda item inversely related to stakes|—|
|Leaky abstraction|Simplification failing under some conditions → exposes complexity it hid|../laws/teslers-law.md|
|Legibility vs readability|Legible text: people can read it; readable text = easy + pleasant to take in|—|
|Leveling and sharpening|Over time memory drops some details + exaggerates others → distorts retrospective reports|—|
|Load types|Intrinsic load = effort tied to goal itself: tracking goals, taking in anything new + holding information it requires. Extraneous load = load design adds + can take away again; from e.g. purposeless elements or ones tugging attention elsewhere; its effort buys no comprehension. Germane load = effort on essential content + schema building; grows as extraneous load falls|../laws/cognitive-load.md|
|Locatedness|Orientation given by working periphery: awareness of nearby state, what recently changed + what comes next, held w/o conscious attention|[Calm technology patterns](../patterns/calm-technology-and-device-etiquette.md)|
|Loss aversion; endowment effect|Losses weigh roughly twice as much as equal gains. Endowment effect = owned things valued above same things when not owned|../laws/cognitive-bias.md|
|Luxury surveillance|Monitoring people pay for + experience as benefit; normalizes surveillance of others|humane-design-ideas.md|
|Mapping|Relation between control + its effect; set out in this sense 1988. Natural mapping = control mirrors result it produces. Hypothetical: hospital bed handset; pushing switch up raises bed's head|—|
|Memory bias|Bias distorting recall; peak-end rule = one|../laws/peak-end-rule.md|
|Mental model|If new system resembles known one → person reapplies picture of known one, i.e. in-the-head account of how known system operates, made of beliefs + stored condensed|../laws/mental-model.md|
|Mental model discordance|Mismatch from abrupt change to well-known product → opt-in periods w/ way to revert ease it|../patterns/departing-from-convention.md|
|Miller's Law|About 7 ± 2 items fit in working memory|../laws/millers-law.md|
|Minimalist instruction|Minimal, task-oriented documentation for people learning by doing|../laws/paradox-of-the-active-user.md|
|Minimum viable complexity|Keeping some meaningful participation/visible work so users feel in control; matters especially for high-stakes actions|../methods/who-absorbs-complexity.md|
|Mismatch|Unit of exclusion: design assumption not fitting someone|../patterns/inclusive-and-accessible-design.md|
|Misrepresented communities|Term preferred over underrepresented or marginalized: those describe people by distance from dominant group; misrepresented names actual problem: others defined these communities narrowly, not letting them define themselves|—|
|Misuse case / abuser persona|Scenario/persona describing how feature could get turned against someone → write misuse cases beside ordinary use cases|../methods/misuse-case-brainstorming.md (how to write them)|
|Multistability, reification, emergence, invariance|Multistability = ambiguous display alternates between mutually exclusive readings, only one at a time; first reading tends to entrench. Hypothetical: thermostat icon reads as either flame or water drop; users see one at a time + tend to keep first. Reification = perceiving more structure than drawn. Hypothetical: four corner brackets around boarding-pass QR code make people see full frame not drawn. Emergence = whole recognized all at once from its outline, before its parts. Invariance = simple shapes recognized across rotation, translation, scaling + restyling|../laws/gestalt-principles-overview.md|
