# Respectful

## In one line
- Respectful = people's well-being, attention + time on loan to product → product must return them w/ value.
- Why it matters: people's overall well-being, attention + time = scarce.
- Governed mainly by how + when product interrupts.
- Main lever = interruption model:
  - each delivery's urgency;
  - recipient's say over manner, timing + sender of notifications;
  - context sensed like considerate person reading room.
- Open when: design/review touches how + when product interrupts, or how it treats people's attention, time + well-being.

## Core practices
Highest weight; check first.
1. Urgency for person decides delivery channel.
2. Notifications off by default, except an essential few.
3. Controls per sender, per category + per channel + quiet hours.
4. Adapt to context + to what people keep ignoring.
5. Keep status at periphery of attention.
6. No manufactured social obligation.
7. No targeting of people's emotional lows.

## Guidance held here (no pattern entry)

### Timing persuasion to a low moment?
- If persuasion, ads or re-engagement would target moments of insecurity, stress or distress → don't do it.
- Why: psychology ethics codes rule out targeting people at moments of emotional vulnerability.
- Case: 2017 document leak showed one platform offering advertisers exactly this targeting of emotional vulnerability.

### Adding one more notification type?
1. Audit total attention load: score every notification type on urgency, frequency, value + whether it arrives on schedule.
2. Review sum of all notifications, not each addition alone.
- Why: attention costs accumulate quietly across features.

## Why it matters
- Interruption pays: instant pings convert better than deferred ones.
- Every product maximizing its own interruptions → tragedy of the attention commons.
- Root = calm technology, term coined in 1990s: information sits at periphery of attention, moves to centre only when it matters, then recedes.
- Purpose of calm design = not merely fewer interruptions; goal = attention returned to room + people in it.
- Original argument for calm design: its gain shows in how far people can achieve genuine presence together, beyond objects around them.
- Its forecast: 21st century where people wield more power; on strength of gain above, calm design may sit at its centre.
- Meaningful connection = what calm design defends + what distraction eats.
- Avoid: pitching calm design as focus/productivity gain → concedes ground to engagement metrics.

## Key concepts
|Term|Meaning|
|---|---|
|Calm technology, 8 principles|least attention; inform + create calm; use the periphery; amplify the best of people + of machines; communicate w/o speaking; work even when failing; minimum technology; respect social norms|
|Locatedness|test for status displays: passes when staying oriented costs no effort + nothing arrives as shock; fails when only constant watching keeps person oriented|
|Etiquette vocabulary|whisper, not shout; inform, not alarm; ask, not assume; gradient softening|
|Interruption cost|reported 25-45 min to recover after disruption, plus anxiety + safety risk. Carry figure as range w/ its reported basis; don't collapse it to one number|
|Fear of missing something|unbounded: felt before disconnecting, rarely borne out afterwards|
|Calm scorecard categories|user experience; notifications + indicators; privacy; well-being + time well spent; support + fail states. Scoring → ../checklists/calm-technology-scorecard.md|

## Audit questions
Scope: well-being, attention + time. All principles' questions → ../checklists/humane-design-audit.md.

|Check|Passes when|
|---|---|
|Urgency to person, not business, decides channel?|sound, vibration or lock-screen only if time-sensitive; otherwise badge, inbox or digest|
|People choose channel, time + sender?|per-sender, per-category + per-channel toggles + quiet hours, not one global switch|
|Emails carry full message body?|no "tap to view" teasers forcing return visits|
|Product adapts to meetings, driving, sleep + focus?|delivery deferred or softened, not identical pings everywhere|
|Could this sit at periphery of attention, not centre?|ambient indicators, tones + subtle badges preferred over modal alerts|
|Read receipts, presence + "seen" indicators opt-in for observed person?|yes; red flag = presence controls held only by observer|
|Product free of rating nags, install banners + forced sign-in to view content?|none present. Why: they serve business + score negatively on well-being|
