# Empowering

## In one line
- Empowering = people's gain from product ranks above product's earnings from them.
- Yardstick = agency, not utility.
- What product gives comes before what it takes; widens what people can do.
- Keep people in charge of when + how much they engage w/ product.
- Open when: design/review touches ranking + personalization, AI decisions, option sets, exit paths, redesigns, or product's success measures.

## Core practices
Highest weight; check first.
1. People can inspect, tune, mute, reset + bypass ranking, e.g. via chronological/following-only view.
2. Hidden options stay discoverable + unpersonalized mode exists.
3. Human in loop for AI decisions; can review, correct, approve + appeal.
4. Product stays invisible until needed.
5. Leaving = as easy as joining.

## Guidance held here (no pattern entry)

### Anticipating what people want?
- Avoid: anticipating needs so far people turn passive.

### Composing a menu or option set?
1. Per screen, write what person came to do.
2. Ask whether offered options serve it.
3. Note anything absent.
- Why: whoever composes option set steers decision.
- Wrong: more options = more agency → Right: more options does not mean more agency.

### Planning a redesign?
- Why it matters: continual rearrangement wipes out familiarity faster than it forms → people can operate product but never get confident enough to control it.
- Do: count redesign's cost to people having already learned product; change only what must.
- Do: keep known route working beside new one for stated period.
- Do: say plainly what moved + why.
- Do: book relearning as time taken from person, not free side effect of shipping.

### People harmed who never use the product?
- Do: give affected communities recourse: people harmed by algorithm w/o ever using product get route to report + contest harm + change algorithm's behaviour.

### Before optimizing for growth?
- Do: test design for honesty, unobtrusiveness + usefulness (classic good-design ethics).
- Respecting people + doing no harm = part of good design's definition.
- Manipulation can work w/o lying.

### Automating a decision?
- Rule: every automated feature fixes ratio of speed gained to human judgement retained → set it consciously.
- Why: automated decisions people cannot question/overturn get distrusted even when usually sound; relationship w/ system degrades in turn.

## Why it matters
- Product can prove enormously useful while draining agency: mined data, shattered focus, narrowed choices.
- Personalization narrows choice: option the algorithm never surfaces = option the person no longer has.
- When personalization hides option, choice made for person w/o telling them.
- Choice architecture can bend people's day-to-day actions toward platform's goals.
- North star: 1962 framework: computers extending what human minds can do, raising people's capability to comprehend + solve problems.

## Key concepts
|Term|Meaning|
|---|---|
|Agency|capacity to see own options + choose among them; diminished when systems decide silently|
|Utility vs empowerment|utility = task completion; empowerment = person keeps setting own life's pace while command of own life grows in confidence + capability|
|Menu control|whoever defines option set shapes decision. Ask: what's absent from menu + whose goals does it serve?|
|Coefficient of friction|judge choice by effort to enact it, not whether it technically exists|
|Co-creation w/ algorithms|people shape ranking, not passively receive it|
|Intelligence augmentation|human, language, artifacts, methods + training form one system, improved together|
|Oversight as basis of trust|automation earns confidence via inspectability + correctability, not via accuracy most of the time|

## Audit questions
Scope: people's gain ahead of product's earnings. All principles' questions → ../checklists/humane-design-audit.md.

|Check|Passes when|
|---|---|
|Can people inspect, tune, mute, reset + bypass ranking?|chronological/following-only view one tap away + "why am I seeing this" on recommendations|
|Privacy + anonymity controls clear, reachable + protective by default?|anonymous/no-account mode where feasible|
|Product stays out of way until summoned?|quiet by default; no constant suggestion surfaces|
|Healthier habits supported by how product shows people own usage patterns?|more than streaks pushing for more use|
|AI decisions: can person understand, review, correct + approve before it takes effect?|legible reasoning + stated confidence + correction path|
|Where personalization narrows view, can people find what got hidden?|full catalogue + unpersonalized mode stay accessible|
|Exit as easy as entry?|cancel, unsubscribe + delete use same channel + step count as sign-up|
|Did anyone ask what people gave up, not only whether task finished?|time, attention, money + privacy spent reported beside completion rates|
