# Team and personal practices

## Use when
- Setting up/checking habits keeping team + each practitioner in it capable of humane choices.
- Team practices: code of ethics, research, language, rationale log, meeting norms, hiring, outside experts, escalation.
- Personal practices: own device use, ethical drift check, holding companies accountable, long repair.

## Team practices

### Code of ethics
- Adopt code of ethics as team.
- Put its questions into design-review template, beside usability questions.

### Research
- Everyone on team, developers included, watches real people use product.
- Keep living portraits of people product serves.
- Test early + continuously.
- Ask why whenever decision rests on opinion or metric-driven request.
- Research what people give up when using product (context, relationships, what it cost them), not only task speed.

### Language + sensitivities
- Retire word "user" from team talk; say "people" instead.
- Replace words like conversion + KPI w/ what happened for person.
- Before ideating, human-sensitivities check: which vulnerability does feature lean on (social approval, reciprocity, variable reward, fear of missing out, menu control, bottomless feeds)? Respects or exploits it? Levers + counters: ../ethics/attention-hijacks.md.

### Rationale log
- Keep tagged, dated design-rationale log. Tags e.g. privacy, security, friction, calm, trust, context, AI, personalization.
- Cross-link related decisions → patterns surface.
- Record value guiding decision beside evidence. Why: two teams handed same result can reasonably decide opposite things because numbers get read through commitments nobody wrote down.
- State what settled decision, e.g.: privacy cost outweighed conversion gain; worst-served group counted more than average; reversible mistake preferred over faster flow. Why: if not recorded, only half of reasoning survives + decision's inheritor gets result w/ judgement stripped out.
- Hypothetical: pharmacy-app team log entry → "<date> · tags: privacy, trust · Question: fold data-sharing consent into one-step sign-up? · Value: people should know what they share · Settled by: privacy cost outweighed the conversion gain → consent stays its own step · See also: reminder opt-in decision."

### Meeting + messaging norms
- Model inside team same respect you design for: agendas shared in advance; silent reading time.
- Laptops + phones out of meetings.
- No messages after hours. No expectation of instant replies.
- Leaders go first in following these norms.

### Hiring + outside experts
- Recruit people w/ lived experience onto team; retain + promote them.
- Hiring interviews: ask candidates how they would respond to user unlike themselves.
- Product touches mental health, elections, children or fragile contexts → bring in outside experts (clinicians, safety organizations, affected communities).

### Escalation
- Know paths: company policies; leadership's stated positions; allies w/ more relative safety; collective statements; legal, media + security basics before raising alarm.
- Raising concerns alone against well-resourced employer rarely succeeds.
- Before taking harm concern beyond your team:
  1. Work out who each internal channel answers to. People / human-resources function exists to protect organization + does not represent employee raising concern.
  2. Keep records + outside contact off company hardware, company accounts + employer-managed personal devices.
  3. Decide what outcome you want.
  4. Weigh personal risk honestly, not heroically.
- Staying + pressing from inside w/ allies = legitimate route, not lesser one.

## Personal practices

### Own device use
- Observe own compulsive vs intentional device use for one week.
- Set personal metrics, e.g. less time, fewer decisions, less forgetting.
- Turn off badges.
- Move low-priority apps off home screen, or launch them by typing.
- Charge phone outside bedroom.
- Go offline daily.
- These = habit re-education, not detox weekend.
- Experience anxiety-inducing interfaces yourself; note what they do to your judgement.

### Ethical drift check
- Every few months ask: goalposts moving a few yards w/ each raise/grant?
- Drift found → correct course or change workplaces.

### Holding companies accountable
- Flag unsolicited messages as spam.
- Leave honest reviews.
- Publicly document deceptive patterns tagged to company, aimed at action, not shaming individual designers. Names to use: ../ethics/deceptive-patterns.md.
- Choose products whose values respect your time + attention.

### The long repair
- Decide now what in your work will need replacing/repairing years from now + its future steward.
